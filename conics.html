The underlying equations

Our cone has half-angle α = arctan(0.62). The plane has a fixed perpendicular distance d from the apex and tilt β:

x² + y² = k² z²,   z cos β − x sin β = d

e = √(1 + k²) · |sin β|,   k = 0.62
