3D Rotations: Order Matters

Noncommutative, but associative. Both sequences rotate the same labeled cube about fixed world axes.

Compare identical angles: the completed sequences generally have different orientations: Rᵧ(θᵧ) Rₓ(θₓ) ≠ Rₓ(θₓ) Rᵧ(θᵧ). Matrix composition nevertheless remains associative: (AB)C = A(BC).
