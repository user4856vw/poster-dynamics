All rotations are active, positive 90° by the right-hand rule, around fixed world axes; the camera and axes do not move.

Top T · Front F · Right R

Same start, different finish: RᵧRₓ ≠ RₓRᵧ (column vectors; rightmost rotation acts first). But (AB)C = A(BC).

All six faces, permanently labeled

T top (yellow), D down (orange), F front (blue), B back (purple), R right (red), L left (green). Opposite pairs are T–D, F–B, R–L.
