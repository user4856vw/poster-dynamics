Cartesian rule

(a + bi)(c + di) = (ac − bd) + (ad + bc)i

Complex multiplication is distributive arithmetic with i² = −1.

Polar rule

(r eⁱᶿ)(s eⁱᶠ) = rs · eⁱ⁽ᶿ⁺ᶠ⁾

Multiply magnitudes; add arguments. Multiplication by i turns a vector 90° counterclockwise without changing its length.
